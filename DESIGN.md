## Brand & Style

This design system channels the energetic, tactile spirit of specialty coffee culture in Oaxaca de Juárez. It bridges authentic neighborhood hospitality with a crisp, contemporary editorial voice—grounded in warmth, visual verve, and approachable ritual.

The target audience spans local daily commuters, creative freelancers, culinary travelers, and discerning specialty coffee seekers looking for exceptional beans without pretension. The emotional atmosphere is bright, caffeinated, and unhurried: warm mornings on stone streets, cold brew condensation on a paper cup, and the playful familiarity of street-level branding.

### Style Directions
- **Editorial Modernism meets Neighborhood Play:** Rich, warm off-white surfaces and creamy tints serve as the foundation, contrasted forcefully against electric Klein / Cobalt blue typography and vibrant spot moments.
- **Typographic Dialect:** Punchy geometric heavyweight sans headers reflect the iconic hand-stamped and sticker-like identity, paired with refined, italicized editorial serifs that evoke curated tasting notes, menu cards, and regional origins.
- **Physicality & Micro-Interactions:** Subtle sticker badge offsets, tactile border stamps, and swift yet elastic micro-interactions celebrate physical coffee merchandise, takeaway cups, and textured menu boards.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate tension between the shock of pure cobalt ink and the organic, tactile warmth of toasted beans, steamed foam, and raw grain paper.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#1526b0` — Klein Cobalt):** The brand’s electric anchor. Used for iconic logotypes, primary CTA pills, live notification badges, dominant headlines, and selected interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#faf8f2` — Warm Crema Canvas):** The overall canvas background. Replaces stark modern whites with the comforting warmth of unbleached paper cups and Oaxacan morning light.
- **Tertiary (`#f6df88` — Mantequilla Yellow):** A soft, playful butter-yellow accent reserved for highlight stickers, promo flags, interactive hovers, and special menu callouts (e.g., “Dirtys” or “Recomendación”).
- **Neutral (`#2a1a14` — Espresso Dark):** A deep, roasted near-black brown. Provides optimal reading contrast for body text, fine metadata, price leaders, and dotted menu dividers without the sterile severity of pure `#000000`.

### Tonal Accents & Functional Roles
- **Paper Accent Surface (`#f1eae0`):** Used for cards, sticky bar backgrounds, inset tiles, and subtle row alternating.
- **Subdued Ink (`#68544a`):** For secondary metadata, single-origin tags, process methods (washed, honey, anaerobic), and disabled states.
- **Cobalt Glow Tint (`rgba(21, 38, 176, 0.08)`): For interactive hover overlays and active pill button selections.

## Typography

The typographical voice uses high-contrast juxtaposition:
1. **Bold Geometric Display (`Epilogue` in weights 700 to 900):** Emulates bold vinyl stickers, takeaway cup sleeves, and strong café window signs. Upper-case usage is encouraged for badges, marquee ribbons, navigation, and top-tier headers.
2. **Editorial Literary Serif (`Newsreader` in regular, medium, and italics):** Inspired by artisanal menu inserts, tasting literature, and conversational quips (such as *“¿Y si antes de chambear pasas por algo de...?”*). Italic cuts bring warmth, narrative pacing, and handcrafted legitimacy.

### Hierarchical Usage
- Use `display-xl` exclusively for hero declarations, large slogan badges, and marquee banners.
- Use `editorial-italic` for subheadings, barista intros, and contextual prompts.
- Use `price-num` alongside dotted tab leaders for drink catalogs to ensure crisp spatial alignment across varied digital screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial column system that mimics printed daily menus and zine layouts, alternating with fluid horizontal scrolls for merchandise and daily pastries.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Elements pack tightly to evoke a handheld pocket menu. Takeout categories scroll along a single edge-bleeding horizontal axis.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1024px`):** 8-column layout. Drink menus split into logical dual columns (Caliente / Frío, Clásicos / De Temporada).
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column layout capped at `1280px` max-width with generous `margin-desktop: 3rem`. Visual balance positions full-bleed photography of iced espresso splashes alongside tight, typographic pricing matrices.

### Spacing Cadence
- Micro rhythm uses `space-xs` and `space-sm` for badge insets and price-to-title locks.
- Medium rhythm uses `space-md` for standard card padding and vertical list gaps.
- Structural layout blocks separate distinct experiences (Menu, Story, Location, Shop) via `space-xl` scaled to double intervals (`5rem`) on larger viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurry digital drop shadows in favor of a tactile, print-oriented elevation hierarchy composed of tonal paper layering, hard stamp offsets, and crisp edge definition.

### Layering Principles
- **Base Canvas:** Deep creamy surface (`#faf8f2`) provides background warmth.
- **Tonal Insets & Cards:** Containers use `#f1eae0` or pure white `#ffffff` with a hairline stroke (`1.5px solid rgba(42, 26, 20, 0.08)`) to maintain physical presence.
- **Sticker / Badge Pop:** High-emphasis callouts, floating order buttons, and active tabs use hard-edged, solid offset drop-shadows: `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #1526b0` or `box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px #2a1a14`. This honors physical stationery, risograph prints, and vinyl cup decals.
- **Overlays & Modals:** Backdrops use a semi-opaque warm espresso wash (`rgba(42, 26, 20, 0.45)`) combined with a light `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp editorial paper cutouts with friendly, organic curves:
- **Buttons & Interactive Pills:** Use fully rounded capsules (`9999px`) to match the soft, tactile feel of bottle caps and coffee stickers.
- **Product Cards & Panels:** Standardize on `roundedness: 2` (base `0.5rem` / `8px` up to `1rem` / `16px` on large cards), evoking thick, rounded kraft-board coasters and die-cut menu boards.
- **Stamp Stickers:** Decorative accent labels and price circles take full circular forms or badge geometries with subtle rotation (`-1.5deg` to `+2deg`) for a human, physical touch.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Action (Klein Pill):** Solid `#1526b0` background, bold uppercase `Epilogue` typography in `#faf8f2`, pill shape (`9999px`), `px: 1.5rem`, `py: 0.75rem`. On hover, shifts up by `-1px` with a crisp butter-yellow or espresso rim effect (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #2a1a14`).
- **Secondary Action (Paper & Border):** Background in `#faf8f2`, text in `#1526b0`, bordered with `2px solid #1526b0`. Hover inverts background to `#1526b0` and text to `#faf8f2`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Action:** Understated serif text in `Newsreader` italic with a dashed bottom border in `#1526b0` for exploratory actions like *“Conoce nuestros granos”*.

### Cards & Menu Listings
- **Drink Menu List Item:** Classical café leader format. Beverage title in bold `Epilogue`, origin/note beneath in `Newsreader` italic, followed by a light dotted dotted leader (`border-bottom: 2px dotted rgba(42, 26, 20, 0.2)`) terminating in dual price columns (`Caliente` / `Frío`) rendered in bold tabular figures.
- **Product & Bag Card:** Off-white container (`#ffffff` or `#f1eae0`), `0.75rem` border radius, encased by a crisp hairline border (`#2a1a14` at 10% opacity). Features an image slot with a floating butter-yellow sticker pill (`#f6df88` with `#2a1a14` text) displaying process details (e.g., “Lavado Pluma Hidalgo”).

### Chips, Pills & Filters
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped selector buttons (`rounded-full`, height 36px) used to switch categories (Bebidas, DIRTYS, Alimentos, Granos). Active state is flooded with `#1526b0` with off-white text; inactive state features transparent cream with `1px solid rgba(42, 26, 20, 0.15)` and espresso text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Warm cream interior (`#ffffff`), `2px solid #2a1a14` on focus, and labels rendered in bold `Epilogue` all-caps (`label-bold`). Placeholder copy appears in muted espresso italic (`#68544a`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom square/circle tokens bordered in `#1526b0` (`2px`), checking triggers a solid `#1526b0` fill with a high-contrast off-white icon.

### Specialized Brand Elements
- **Marquee Ticker:** A continuous Klein-blue banner running across sections with punchy uppercase declarations: `MUCHO CAFÉ • DE TODOS LOS DÍAS • OAXACA DE JUÁREZ • COLD BREW • MOKA TRADICIÓN`.
- **Address & Hours Footer Stamp:** Framed in a bold dark-blue block mirroring the printed receipt style, featuring clear typographic data, WhatsApp order anchors, and physical street markers (Manuel Doblado 202D, Centro).
